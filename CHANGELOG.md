### Stage 02 — ownership, physical identity and persistence

- Persistent colony UUIDs, nonoverlapping dimension-scoped territories, OWNER/MANAGER/VIEWER rights, citizen UUID/entity UUID/binding epoch, lifecycle, needs and remaining active-time timers. Only the explicit operator command creates a physical citizen; unload never creates a replacement.
- Persistent humanoid citizens have actual nine-slot Minecraft inventories and vanilla ground navigation without wandering goals. Authorized owners/managers open the existing inventory through a vanilla menu; every click rechecks current rights and binding. Duplicate, stale and unrecorded embodiments retain their items in quarantine.
- Commands: `colony create <name> <from> <to>`, `member set <colony UUID> <player> <manager|viewer|none>`, `owner set <colony UUID> <player>`, `citizen create <colony UUID> <pos>`, `citizen assign <citizen UUID> <namespaced profession>`, and `status <colony UUID>`, all below `/colonyloom`. Player arguments resolve standard Minecraft profiles; professions builder/courier/carpenter are loaded and validated as one bounded definition set, not executed jobs yet.
- Explicit schema-1 SavedData DTOs use bounded preflight reads before attachment. Corrupt/newer schemas disable mutations without replacing the file. Unknown records retain their original NBT; identifiable dependent colonies are blocked, unrelated colonies are not. Known IDs are checked even when their records are retained because a dependency is unknown.
- A verified dirty session marker precedes mutation. Normal shutdown flushes Minecraft, forces/rereads the exact DTO, then writes a matching clean checkpoint. A dirty/undefined previous marker persistently blocks affected colonies; later clean shutdown alone does not clear that block.
- Operator-only `recovery inspect`, `recovery bind` and `recovery accept-world` expose/revalidate identities and actual items. Bind requires every known competing embodiment loaded and advances the chosen epoch without deleting competitor property. Accept rejects wrong checkpoints, unloaded/stale observations and changed physical inventory; successful acceptance creates/removes no items.
- Exercised dedicated scenario: two colonies, three physical citizens, roles/professions and exact inventories; stale duplicate quarantine, unloaded competitor refusal, epoch-advancing bind, then clean restart with the same UUIDs/epochs/property. Dirty-marker fixture remained blocked through a subsequent clean stop/restart; incomplete/stale/changed-inventory acceptance was refused before final property-preserving acceptance.
- Exercised actual graphical client: humanoid rendering and nine-slot inventory; integrated-world provisioning and a separate integrated world with both prior colony IDs unknown, all three prior entity UUIDs absent and zero citizen entities. Actual corrupt-save startup, command refusal and clean shutdown retained all 86 fixture bytes. This stage does not establish scheduled work, economy execution or NPC scale.
- Local evidence: `runs/server/colonyloom-identity-02b/colonyloom-identity-observations.jsonl`, integrated worlds' matching observation logs, and `build/client-stage02-citizens.png`, `client-stage02-menu-inventory.png`, `client-stage02-integrated-setup.png`, `client-stage02-isolation.png`, under `colonyloom-neoforge`. Disposable worlds/evidence are excluded from the JAR.

### Stage 03 — bounded scheduling and lossless wakeups

- Server-owned admission ledger reserves accepted state and cancel/recovery capacity atomically. Separate memory lanes protect critical/service capacity; accumulated CPU deficits distribute 50/10/40 critical/service/normal quanta and lend idle CPU shares. Registered colonies retain admission guarantees; lowering limits drains without deleting accepted records.
- Typed persistent work lifecycle, exclusive indexed worker assignments (at most eight candidates), active-time residuals, priority/aging and colony round-robin. Bounded ready heaps, retained dirty debt, revision rechecks and indexed deadlines prevent lost wakeups under saturation; transient queues rebuild after restore.
- Commands: `/colonyloom work wait <colony UUID> <active ticks>`, `work cancel <work UUID>` and `work priority <work UUID> <0..10>`. Manager authority applies; wait work executes real active time, not construction. `status` includes global admission usage, limits and temporary excess. Recovery acceptance cancels unfinished work and releases assignments without changing property.
- NeoForge SERVER `config/colonyloom-server.toml` validates immutable snapshots on the owning server thread; invalid numeric snapshots retain the previous validated profile. Conservative default: 30 citizens, 2048 works, 1024 ready entries, 8192 wait registrations and a 5 ms managed guard. Supported decode ceilings are not measured scale claims.
- Verified core regressions: two-colony progress with one normal ready slot, during-step invalidation without another event, cancellation at saturated wait capacity, exclusive assignments/retirement and no offline or UNKNOWN-readiness timer catch-up. Seven physical persistence GameTests passed, including compressed timer residual restore and terminal non-reexecution.
- Actual dedicated and graphical integrated worlds advanced two timers, checkpointed partial residuals, restarted, cancelled old work and completed both 40-tick replacement works with zero remaining time/assignments. Dedicated final DTO retained three citizens and four terminal records with a matching clean marker. Live TOML smoke retained capacity 2 after invalid 0, refused citizen creation with `STATE_LIMIT`, then applied 30 without losing accepted citizens.
- Initial dedicated smoke exited 1 without a JVM exception/shutdown record; cause remains undetermined. Its dirty marker was preserved and the normal operator recovery scenario accepted the physically observed world before the successful dedicated rerun. No marker bypass or replacement citizens.
- Local evidence: dedicated/integrated `colonyloom-identity-observations.jsonl`, `build/client-stage03-timer-resume.png`, `client-stage03-config.png` and `client-stage03-dedicated.png`. Movement, economy and 300-citizen capacity are not established by this stage.

### Stage 04 — physical claims, chunks and movement

- Server-owned chunk admission, indexed target claims and revision-guarded navigation share the existing global budgets. Persisted inclusive dimension/AABB claims restore conflicts as blocked; recovery acceptance releases inspected claims. Desired chunk requests survive safe eviction; protected physical/cargo/critical regions are not evicted.
- NeoForge `colonyloom:runtime` tickets use colony UUID ownership and shared references; startup validation removes stale tickets before fresh admission. Conservative accounting charges the union of 5×5 loaded rings, 3×3 block-ticking rings and entity-ticking centers. Source default loaded footprint is 200 so the one-eighth service reserve can hold one isolated 25-chunk request; existing TOMLs are not silently migrated.
- `/colonyloom work move <colony UUID> <pos>` creates persistent physical movement work. Exact citizen epoch/assignment/readiness, request and goal revisions, admitted chunks and chunk revisions guard vanilla ground search and every movement tick. Maximum target range is 64 blocks; obstruction retry intervals are 20/40/80/160/200 ticks. Cancellation stops the old route without teleporting or replacing the citizen.
- Inactive embodiments remain identifiable but cannot execute navigation. Admission pauses their own clock without catch-up and releases ordinary assignments; vanilla damage and actual inventory remain active. Guarded path classification preserves the loader-visible context position update, including on path-type cache hits.
- Verified build/core regressions and 14 physical GameTests: real walking, cancellation, second-route arrival, signed chunk-boundary routes, compressed movement/claim restore, 60 inactive ticks without own-clock accumulation, physical damage/property preservation, actual remote 25/9/1 ticket rings with one shared owner released, and protected A+C→A+B→A+C dependency swaps at footprint 50.
- Connected graphical Dev exercised the public dedicated move command and completion check; exact carpenter UUID/epoch and six oak logs survived. Normal client/server exits were 0; final DTO has three citizens, completed movement, no assignments and the matching clean marker. Native screenshot capture was black and foreground activation refused; no visual movement claim is made.
- Observed synchronous high-water calls across successful physical runs: chunk ticket up to 72.9238 ms, vanilla search up to 53.2068 ms. These are not p99 distributions or a 300-citizen capacity claim; stage 06 must measure the platform barrier and replace the search backend if required.
- Local evidence: dedicated `colonyloom-identity-observations.jsonl` and final SavedData/session marker; generated GameTest and server logs. Disposable fixtures remain excluded from the release JAR.

### Stage 05 — physical construction and recovery

- `/colonyloom build <colony UUID> colonyloom:stair_strip <pos> <0|90|180|270>` admits one compact sixteen-block construction goal and exact target claim. Builders walk between transformed work-origin waypoints, use actual NPC-menu materials, wait for missing items and preserve placed blocks/property on cancellation. No warehouse economy or virtual buffer is introduced here.
- Bounded vanilla StructureTemplate NBT plus JSON metadata supplies immutable block/item descriptors and canonical pinned digests. Pins share compact palettes, retain accepted versions through reload and enforce 64-version/64-MiB limits. Test-only four-stair resources use the same format; production markers are `work_origin=(0,0,1)` and `delivery_buffer=(-1,0,0)`.
- Real item use borrows the NPC slot stack through a nonsaved survival actor with the current owner's profile, without operator bypass or a copied resource source. Current individual authority, target claim, native state, real expense and entity-ticking footprint are rechecked; cancellable NeoForge interaction/placement events can veto without expense. Unexpected physical results become AMBIGUOUS; no compensation or replay.
- Placement/death effect evidence is gated by the dirty session marker. Recovery inspection includes actual target states and a whole-site world digest; accept-world revalidates observations, preserves physical reality and closes the old goal. Unknown pins/effects retain original records and dependent obligations. Verified checkpoint compaction keeps PREPARED/AMBIGUOUS evidence, open goals and referenced pins; NeoForge pending IO is drained before DTO verification/forced replacement.
- Verified dedicated crash runner: clean two-block stop/restart continues to four exact blocks with zero NPC material; cancellation retains two blocks/two stairs; before/after-change JVM halts preserve independently saved sides, block recovery, spend/issue nothing for 100 ticks and never replay after accept-world. Production death retains four real dropped stairs and an OBSERVED durable effect. Live threshold maintenance reduces 2048 witnesses to the retained PREPARED witness while preserving the open site and durable cursor.
- All 33 physical GameTests passed, including cancellation inside the post-effect callback: one real block/one actual item expense remain, cursor stays uncommitted and recovery blocks. Core death regression releases an assigned physical work without rewriting its stage to timer.
- Actual integrated client invoked the public sixteen-block goal, rendered the builder beside placed wooden stairs, verified exactly four target blocks/twelve remaining air positions and empty nine-slot NPC inventory, then cancelled through the public command. OpenGL screenshots `runs/client/screenshots/colonyloom-construction-{120,300,600}.png` show the real world; prior desktop black captures are not used as proof.
- A real Dev graphical client connected to the dedicated server; the same public sixteen-block build reached MATERIALS wait with four real stairs and zero inventory, then public cancellation preserved the physical result. Observations: `runs/server/colonyloom-identity-02b/colonyloom-identity-observations.jsonl`.
- Evidence: `build/reports/colonyloom/construction-1790965663894` (four dedicated scenarios) and `construction-1790966103790` (live maintenance/death), under `colonyloom-neoforge`. Fixture servers, observers, worlds and reports remain excluded from release.
